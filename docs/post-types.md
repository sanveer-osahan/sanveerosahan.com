# Technical post types

Four formats for a technical post, each with its own shape. Pick the one that
fits the goal before drafting. Adapted from BlackGirlBytes, "The Ultimate Guide
to Writing Technical Blog Posts"
(https://dev.to/blackgirlbytes/the-ultimate-guide-to-writing-technical-blog-posts-5464).

## 1. How-to

Step-by-step guide that walks the reader through one specific task.

Use it when you're teaching a concrete procedure.

Shape:
1. Introduction: hook and the problem being solved.
2. The steps, each with an explanation and any supporting links.
3. Conclusion: recap, final thoughts, call to action.

## 2. Explainer

Introduces and explains a concept. No task to complete, just understanding.

Use it when the reader needs the foundation before anything else makes sense.

Shape:
1. Introduction: hook, what the topic is, why it matters.
2. Body: definition, examples, why it's important.
3. Conclusion: summary, closing thoughts, call to action.

## 3. Thought leadership / opinion

Shares a perspective on where the industry is going.

Use it when you have a stance worth adding to the conversation.

Shape:
1. Introduction: hook, the opinion, why it's relevant now.
2. Supporting points, each backed by evidence or an example.
3. Counter-arguments, if they strengthen the case.
4. Conclusion: restate the position, call to action.

## 4. Listicle

Related items in a numbered or bulleted list, built for quick reading.

Use it when the value is a collection and skimmability is the point.

Shape:
1. Introduction: hook and the theme tying the list together.
2. The list items, each with a short explanation and example.
3. Conclusion: final thoughts, call to action.

## Note for the AI coding agents post

It's a blend. The setup walk-through is a **how-to**, the "Agent = Model +
Harness" section is an **explainer**, and the tokens-per-second and quality
comparison carries an **opinion**. Lead with the how-to spine and fold the
other two in where they support it, rather than switching format mid-post.

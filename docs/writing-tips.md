# Technical blog writing tips

Reference for writing articles. Technical writing is taking a complex coding or
engineering topic and breaking it into something a reader can follow and use.
It builds a personal brand, sharpens your own understanding through teaching,
and hands the community a working solution.

## Best practices

- **Define your audience.** Beginner or experienced. Pick one and pitch every
  explanation at that level. When in doubt, write for the reader who knows less.
- **Conversational but concise.** Talk to the reader like a peer, not a
  textbook. Short paragraphs. Plain words. Get to the point.
- **Format for skimming.** Most readers scan before they read. Use headings,
  bullets, numbered lists, and bold to carry the structure on their own.
- **Show code that earns its place.** Concrete, readable blocks with syntax
  highlighting. Include only the lines that prove the point. Cut the rest.

## The writing process

1. **Title and cover.** Grab attention and make the core idea clear at a glance.
   No clickbait. The reader should know what they're getting.
2. **Introduction.** State the problem, the prerequisites, and what the reader
   will walk away knowing. Set the payoff up front.
3. **Body.** Walk through the solution step by step. Lead with concrete
   examples and explain the reasoning. Show the why behind the code, not just
   the code.
4. **Conclusion.** Recap the main points and invite the reader to respond or
   ask questions.

## Voice reminders

- Vary sentence length so the rhythm sounds human.
- One idea per paragraph. Break early.
- State the thing and stop. Trust the reader.

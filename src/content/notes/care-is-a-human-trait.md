AI Coding Agents are not capable enough to build a delightful experience for end users. They just spit out code and lack that human touch.

When building for humans, the core aspect to earn their trust is care. Care is a human trait and nothing will be able to take that away from us.

The positive side to using the Coding Agents:

A few years ago, when I tried building some products as a side project, being a backend developer, I struggled with frontend. Got overwhelmed with what JavaScript library to learn — React, Angular, Vue, Next — instead of focusing on the build.

Now the Coding Agents can write the frontend for me. And I can focus on what really matters — building a delightful experience for my users.

Whether you are a backend, frontend, full stack, mobile, or an AI engineer, don't forget to put that human touch of "care" in your work. That will drive you to be better, to grow, not only in your career, but also in your life.

Care is both a deliberate action and an emotion so strong no AI model or harness will be able to capture it.

Staying on track is easy when you're having a good day.
Bad days? That's when discipline feels like punishment.

When too many projects need attention at once. When multiple fires hit simultaneously. Most people abandon their routine to firefight. They justify breaking commitments to "survive."

Pushing through this pain line is where you get growth. That's how you know you're committed to something beyond comfort.

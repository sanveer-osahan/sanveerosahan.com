When building a product, do not wait for others to find basic issues, least of all the customer. Don't leave them for your team to clean up either. Build it, own it, and validate it.

Here is a simple routine I follow:

Build the "happy path" toward the end of the day. End your day on a high note, confident that you have built a world-class feature, and get a solid 7 - 8 hours of sleep.

The next morning, wake up and step entirely into the shoes of your user. If you encounter an annoying experience, do not worry, your builder persona is there to fix it.

The more effectively you can switch between these personas, the better the final product experience will be. Your user persona must never carry the biases of your builder persona.

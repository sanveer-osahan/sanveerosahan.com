When I was young in my software engineering career, I thought writing good, bug-free code was the most important thing that made a business run.

With time, I realized building great systems that scale is what actually matters more.

Then as a senior engineer and mentor, I learned that building a great team and helping them ship fast is the ultimate driver of business growth.

But when I stepped into building my own business, I now understand that sales is what creates and sustains a business so the engineers can get to write good code, build scalable systems, and build great teams.
